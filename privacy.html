Privacy policy

Worth The Trip? · Last updated 11 October 2026

In short: there is no account, no advertising and no tracking, and your location stays on your phone. Most of what the app stores stays on your iPhone, and deleting the app removes it.

Worth The Trip ("the app") helps you find the cheapest fuel near you in the UK and whether it's worth the drive. This policy explains what the app does with information. The app is independent: it is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, GOV.UK or Fuel Finder.

Who is responsible

Worth The Trip is made by an independent developer in the UK, trading as Worth The Trip, who is responsible for how the app handles information. You can contact us at worththetrip.support@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The app stores the following only on your device, to make it work:

  • Your settings, favourite stations, filters, vehicle details and saved cars, loyalty discounts, price alerts, and saved places (Home, Work and recent searches).
  • If you add the widget, a small file with the name, price and distance of the best value station near you is saved in a folder shared between the app and the widget on your phone. It does not contain your location, and it is never uploaded.
  • If you use the fill-up log, the fills you log: date, station, litres, price and, if you choose, mileage. Also notes you write about stations, and any stations you have reported and chosen to hide for a day.
  • Your location, while you use the app, to measure distances to stations. The app also remembers where it last saw you, so that price alerts can check the right area while the app is closed.
  • A history of fuel prices the app has seen near you, used to show whether prices are rising or falling.
  • Whether you have Pro, so the app knows at start-up without waiting for the network.

You can erase all of this by deleting the app.

What leaves your phone

  • Fuel prices. The app downloads fuel prices and station details for the whole of the UK, then works out what is near you on your phone. Your location is not sent with that request. The request goes to a server run by the developer (hosted on Cloudflare), which gets the data from the UK government's Fuel Finder service. As with any internet request, the server and Cloudflare can see your device's IP address and may keep ordinary technical logs. We do not use these to identify or track you. The app also downloads the government's weekly UK average pump prices and tax rates, and, if you have Pro, a summary of recent price changes, from the same server. Both are the same for everyone.
  • Place searches. If you search for a town, postcode or place, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps (MapKit) to find it. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • The trip planner, trip cost calculator and your commute. If you use the first two, your start and destination are sent to Apple Maps (MapKit) to work out the route. If you save Home and Work and leave the commute card on (Settings, Commute), your Home and Work are sent to Apple Maps the same way, once, and again only if either changes; you can switch the card off at any time. The route and the stations along it are then worked out on your phone. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Directions. When you ask for directions to a station, its location is passed to Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze, whichever you chose. Those apps have their own privacy policies.
  • Bank holidays. The app occasionally downloads the list of UK bank holidays from GOV.UK.
  • Purchases. Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and manages subscriptions. We never see your card or payment details, and we do not receive your name or email address from Apple. The app only checks with Apple whether you own Pro.
  • Reports. If you report a wrong price or a closed station, the app opens your email app with a message to us already written: the station's name, address and ID, the price the app showed, what you tell us you saw, and the app version. Nothing is sent unless you press Send, and your location is not included. We use it only to look into the problem, keep it as long as it is useful, and delete it on request.
  • If you email us. We receive your email address and your message, and use them only to reply. We keep the conversation for as long as it is useful to help you or to improve the app, and delete it on request.

What the app does not do

  • It does not require an account.
  • It does not collect your name, email address or contact details. (We only have these if you email us.)
  • It does not show advertising and contains no third-party analytics or tracking.
  • It does not sell or share personal information.

Notifications

Price alert notifications are created on your phone. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings. The app asks for permission only when you save your first alert.

Your choices and rights

  • You can refuse or withdraw location permission in iOS Settings. The app still works if you search for a place instead.
  • You can delete the app to remove everything it stored on your phone.
  • Because the app collects almost nothing about you, there is usually nothing for us to look up or delete. If you have emailed us, you can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold, by emailing the address above.
  • If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Data source

Fuel price information contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Source: UK Fuel Finder. Prices can be out of date or wrong, so always check the pump price.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
